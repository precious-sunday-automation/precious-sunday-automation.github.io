How I got here

I started on the front line, answering customers at a bank's contact center. Then I moved into quality, scoring interactions and checking compliance. From that seat, I could see exactly where time and quality were being lost.

Now I build automations that fix those problems, and I bring a marketing mindset to them: the point is a better result for the business, not a clever workflow.

From the front line of customer care, to quality, to automation.
